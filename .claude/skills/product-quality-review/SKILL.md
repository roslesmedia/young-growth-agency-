# Product quality review

Produce `09-qa-report.md` with pass/fail per item and fixes made.

## Content
- [ ] Delivers the promise on the sales page (every promised item exists)
- [ ] Quick win present early; every chapter/module ends with an action
- [ ] Facts/stats sourced; no fabricated quotes; claims compliant (no guaranteed income/health results)
- [ ] Creator's voice throughout; AI filler removed; creator stories included
- [ ] Spelling/grammar pass (read aloud key sections); consistent terminology, capitalization, numbers

## Design
- [ ] Matches creator brand kit (colors, fonts, imagery); consistent spacing and heading hierarchy
- [ ] No orphan headings, cut-off tables, blank pages, low-res images, text over busy images
- [ ] Cover readable as a small thumbnail
- [ ] Contrast sufficient (body text ≥ 4.5:1); minimum body 10.5pt in PDFs

## Technical
- [ ] All links work (TOC internal links, external URLs, template copy/duplicate links, upsell links with UTM)
- [ ] Fonts embedded (`pdffonts`), file size reasonable, file named `Product-Name-by-Creator.pdf`
- [ ] Opens correctly on iPhone, Android, iPad, desktop (Apple Books/Preview, Acrobat, Chrome)
- [ ] Digital planner links tested in GoodNotes/Notability if applicable
- [ ] Videos: audio levels, captions, lesson order, thumbnails

## Delivery
- [ ] Test purchase end-to-end (checkout → email → download/access) incl. order bump and upsell
- [ ] Delivery email copy in creator's voice with support contact
- [ ] Refund policy and terms visible; copyright notice & disclaimer page in product

## Approval
- [ ] Creator reviewed and approved final files (written confirmation)
