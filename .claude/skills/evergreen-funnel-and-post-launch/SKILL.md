# Evergreen funnel & post-launch growth

## Evergreen funnel
Content (reels/carousels with keyword CTA) → DM automation → free lead magnet (mini version of the product: checklist, 1 chapter, 3-day challenge) → 5–7 email nurture → offer with personal deadline (e.g. 72h discount via Deadline Funnel-type tooling or platform coupons with expiry) → buyer onboarding → upsell.

## Always-on sales moves
- Link-in-bio: product first, clear outcome name, price visible.
- Story highlight for the product (what's inside, reviews, FAQ).
- 1–2 product mentions per week in content (soft CTAs at end of value posts).
- Pinned posts: best-performing launch reel + testimonial.
- Quarterly or seasonal promos: New Year, back to school, summer, Black Friday/Cyber Monday (often a creator's biggest week), creator's birthday/anniversary.

## Relaunch calendar
Relaunch 2–4× per year with a new angle, new bonus, or v2 — relaunches often beat the first launch because of more testimonials and a bigger email list.

## Value ladder
Free lead magnet → $7–27 tripwire/mini product → $27–97 core product → $97–497 course/bundle → $500+ coaching/community/VIP. Each product's thank-you page and onboarding emails point to the next step.

## Product v2
Collect buyer questions and feedback → add chapters, templates, video walkthroughs → announce "free update for existing buyers" (great re-engagement and a reason to post).

## Affiliates
Invite buyers and peer creators to promote for 30–50% commission; give them swipe copy, graphics (designed in the creator's style) and codes.
