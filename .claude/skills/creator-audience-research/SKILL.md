# Creator audience research

Goal: know the audience better than they know themselves, in their own words. This document feeds product ideas, chapter titles, sales copy and hooks.

## Sources (in priority order)
1. **Creator's comments** — top 20 posts; copy every question, request, complaint, "I struggle with…". Ask creator for **DM screenshots** and **story Q&A/poll results**.
2. **Run a research story sequence** (ask creator to post): poll "Do you struggle with X? yes/no" → question sticker "What's your #1 struggle with X?" → slider "How badly do you want Y?" → "Would you want a guide on X?".
3. **Survey** (Typeform/Google Forms) to email list / link in bio: biggest struggle, what they've tried, what they'd pay for, preferred format (PDF/video/template/community), budget.
4. **Reddit** (`site:reddit.com <niche> struggling`), YouTube comments on top niche videos, **Amazon 1–3★ reviews** of niche books ("I wish it had…"), Quora, Facebook groups.
5. **Competitor products**: Gumroad/Stan/Etsy/Payhip search, other creators' link-in-bio offers. Record name, format, price, page count/modules, promise, reviews, gaps.

## Extraction framework
| Bucket | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Pains | specific frustrations, in quotes |
| Desires / dream outcome | what success looks like, timeframe |
| Failed attempts | what they tried and why it didn't work |
| Objections | "no time", "too expensive", "won't work for me", "I'm a beginner" |
| Exact language | phrases repeated 3+ times → use verbatim in copy |
| Awareness level | unaware / problem-aware / solution-aware / product-aware |
| Buying triggers | events that make them buy now (season, new year, job hunt, wedding, summer) |
| Segments | beginner vs intermediate, budget, life situation |

## Output (`03-audience-research.md`)
```
## Who they are (1 paragraph persona + segments)
## Top 10 pains (ranked by frequency) — with 2 verbatim quotes each
## Dream outcome & timeframe
## Objections & how to answer them
## Language bank (30+ phrases to reuse)
## Competitor landscape (table: product | price | format | promise | gap)
## Product opportunities (gaps we can own)
```
Always cite where quotes came from; never invent quotes. Mark inferences as inferences.
