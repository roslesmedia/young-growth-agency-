# Launch metrics & reporting

## UTM convention
`utm_source=<instagram|tiktok|email|youtube|ads>` `utm_medium=<reel|story|carousel|bio|dm|newsletter|paid>` `utm_campaign=<product-slug>-launch-<yyyymm>` `utm_content=<post-id or keyword>`. Discount codes per channel as backup attribution (in-app browsers and DMs lose UTMs).

## KPIs
| Metric | Formula | Typical healthy range (creator launches, rough) |
|---|---|---|
| Sales page conversion | purchases ÷ unique visitors | 2–8% warm traffic (higher for waitlist) |
| Waitlist conversion | waitlist buyers ÷ waitlist size | 10–30% |
| Email click rate | clicks ÷ delivered | 2–8% |
| AOV | revenue ÷ orders | price × (1 + bump/upsell lift) |
| Bump take rate | bumps ÷ orders | 20–40% |
| Upsell take rate | upsells ÷ orders | 5–20% |
| Revenue per follower | launch revenue ÷ followers | benchmark across creators |
| Refund rate | refunds ÷ orders | < 5% |
| DM automation CTR | link clicks ÷ DMs sent | 30–60% |
Use ranges as orientation, not promises; compare creator against their own previous launches.

## Daily war room (during open cart)
Revenue today/total vs target • orders by source/code • page visits & conversion • top 5 posts by link clicks/comments • email performance • objections seen in DMs • action items for tomorrow.

## Revenue split statement
Gross sales − platform & payment fees − refunds − (agreed costs, e.g. ad spend) = net → split per agreement. Share a monthly statement with order export attached.

## Post-launch report (`11-launch-report.md`)
Summary (revenue, orders, conversion, AOV) • what worked (best angles, posts, emails, days) • what didn't • buyer feedback themes • product improvements for v2 • evergreen plan and relaunch date. Use the dataviz skill for charts if presenting.
