# Launch email sequences

## Sequences & timing
**Waitlist welcome (immediately):** confirm + what's coming + founder perk + reply prompt ("hit reply and tell me your #1 struggle with X") → boosts deliverability and gives research.

**Pre-launch (T-5 → T-1, 3–4 emails):** story of why the creator made it → the core problem & mistake → quick win/teaching → "tomorrow at 9am, early access opens".

**Open cart (5–8 emails over 3–7 days):**
1. Doors open (waitlist early access): what it is, who for, founder price, deadline, link.
2. Public open / "it's live".
3. Story/case study: transformation (creator or early buyer).
4. What's inside (detailed) + bonuses.
5. FAQ / objection crusher.
6. Proof: buyer reactions/testimonials.
7. 24h left (urgency, recap).
8. Final hours (short, 2–3 lines) — often the highest-revenue email; send 2 on close day (morning + 2–3h before).
Exclude buyers from remaining sales emails (segment by purchase tag).

**Cart abandonment (if platform supports):** 1h and 24h after initiate-checkout.

**Buyer onboarding:** access + quick start (day 0) → first win check-in (day 2) → testimonial/review request (day 7–14) → upsell (day 10+).

**Lead-magnet nurture (evergreen):** deliver freebie → creator story → teaching → soft pitch → proof → offer with deadline.

## Writing rules
Plain-text style (looks personal), from the creator's name, one CTA per email, subject lines 3–7 words in voice (curiosity/benefit/urgency), preview text set, first line hooks, short paragraphs, P.S. restates offer. Deadline emails state exact time + timezone.

## Subject line patterns
"it's finally here", "the mistake I made for 3 years", "<first name>, your early access", "this closes tonight", "can I be honest?", "what's inside (peek)".

## Deliverability
Authenticate domain (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) on the creator's email platform, warm up cold lists with value emails, avoid image-heavy designs and spammy words, include unsubscribe and physical address (CAN-SPAM/GDPR).
