# Instagram/TikTok DM automation funnels

Comment-keyword automation boosts reach (comments are an engagement signal) and converts warm viewers at the moment of highest interest.

## Tools
ManyChat (Meta-approved partner; Instagram + Messenger + WhatsApp; TikTok support where available), or similar Meta-approved tools. Creator connects their professional IG account. Check current platform rules and tool capabilities.

## Core flow (comment keyword → DM → link/email)
1. Post with CTA "Comment **GUIDE** and I'll send it to you".
2. Trigger: comment contains keyword → public auto-reply variants ("Check your DMs 💌", rotate 3–5 variants to look natural).
3. DM 1 (in creator voice): "Hey! Here's the link 👇" + button. Optionally ask "Want me to send it to your email too?" → collect email → sync to email platform with tag (waitlist / lead magnet / buyer intent).
4. Follow-up DM after 23h (inside Meta's 24h messaging window) if they didn't click: short reminder/objection answer.
5. Tag subscribers by keyword/post for later segmentation.

## Launch use-cases
| Phase | Keyword | Delivers |
|---|---|---|
| Waitlist | EARLY | waitlist signup + founder price promise |
| Lead magnet | FREE / CHECKLIST | free mini guide → email nurture → product |
| Open cart | LINK / GUIDE | sales page with UTM per post |
| Close | LAST | reminder with deadline |
Story stickers: "Reply 🔥 for the link" triggers on story replies.

## Copy rules
Short, human, creator voice, one link per message, include what they get and why now. Avoid spammy all-caps; don't promise outcomes.

## Compliance & best practice
Follow Meta's messaging policies (24-hour window, no unsolicited bulk messaging); "follow to receive" gating can hurt trust — use sparingly; keep the opt-out clear; respect privacy for collected emails (privacy policy link).

## Metrics
Comments per post, DM open/click rate, email capture rate, sales per keyword (UTM `utm_content=<keyword>`).
