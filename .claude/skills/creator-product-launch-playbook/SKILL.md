# Creator product launch playbook

## Timeline (adapt to product size)
| Phase | When | Goal | Key actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Prep | T-21 → T-14 | assets ready | product done, sales page, checkout, automations, content batch filmed, emails written |
| 1. Warm-up (seed) | T-14 → T-7 | make audience feel the problem & see the creator's method | 5–8 value posts on the product topic, behind-the-scenes "I'm making something", polls/question stickers, story the creation process |
| 2. Waitlist | T-7 → T-1 | collect hot leads | "Comment KEYWORD for early access + founder discount" reels/carousels; waitlist page; stories daily; tease covers/pages |
| 3. Open cart | Day 0–3 | sales spike | waitlist gets 24h early access + best price → public launch; launch reel + carousel + 6–12 stories/day; live Q&A; emails; DM automations; share buyer reactions |
| 4. Mid-launch | Day 3–5 | sustain | testimonials/first wins, objection-busting content, FAQ stories, case studies, behind-the-scenes |
| 5. Close | final 48h | urgency | "price goes up / bonuses end tonight" stories every few hours, last-chance email ×2–3, final live |
| 6. Post-launch | +1 week | convert stragglers, deliver | thank-you, buyer onboarding, collect testimonials, move to evergreen |
Small/impulse products (< $30) can use a compressed 7–10 day version; evergreen sales continue after.

## Story sequences (the #1 sales driver for creators)
Launch-day example (6–10 frames): face-to-camera "it's finally here" → the problem → what's inside (screen recording scroll) → who it's for → founder price + deadline → link sticker → poll "Did you grab it?" → reshare buyer DMs. Repeat with different angles daily: story, FAQ, demo, proof, objection, urgency.

## Creator responsibilities (agree upfront)
Film launch reels/stories (we script), go live 1–3 times, reply to DMs/comments (or approve automation replies), share buyer messages, post on schedule. Provide a shot list and daily brief.

## Agency responsibilities
Product, sales page, checkout, automations, emails, content scripts and designs (in creator's style), posting calendar, daily metrics, real-time optimization (double down on the best-converting angle/post).

## Daily launch war room
Check: sales by channel/code, page conversion rate, top posts by link clicks, DM automation volume, objections in DMs → turn top objection into tomorrow's content.

## Contingencies
Low sales day 1 → more face-to-camera stories, a live, a bonus for next 24h, boost the best reel with ads (paid-ads-for-digital-products). Tech issue → transparent story + extended deadline.

## Output (`10-launch-plan.md`)
Calendar table (date, platform, format, angle, asset owner, status), story scripts, email schedule, automation keywords, KPIs and targets.
