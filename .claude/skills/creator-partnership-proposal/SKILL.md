# Creator partnership proposal

## Outreach DM / email (personal, short, specific)
```
Hey <name> — been following your <topic> content, the <specific post> one blew up for a reason.
I noticed tons of your comments asking for <recurring request> and there's nothing in your bio to buy yet.
We're Young Growth Agency — we build and launch digital products for creators (we do the research,
writing, design, sales page and launch plan; you bring the expertise and audience).
Would you be open to a 15-min call? I already sketched 3 product ideas for your audience.
```
Follow up twice (day 3, day 7) with value: a mock cover or 3 product ideas in a one-pager.

## Discovery call (20 min)
1. Their goals (income, freedom, authority) and past product attempts.
2. Audience questions they get most.
3. Time they can commit (filming, lives, stories during launch week).
4. Present 3 ideas + rough revenue scenario (followers × reach × conversion × price).
5. Explain process & timeline (2–6 weeks to launch). 6. Next step: proposal within 48h.

## Revenue scenario math (show conservative/base/optimistic)
`Buyers ≈ engaged audience reached during launch × conversion`. Example: 80k followers, launch content reaches ~40k unique, 1–3% buy → 400–1,200 buyers × $37 = $14.8k–$44.4k. Always label as estimates, not promises.

## Deal structures
| Model | When | Typical shape |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue share | agency takes risk, creator has audience | agency 30–50% of net product revenue; agency covers creation/launch work |
| Setup fee + smaller share | bigger creators / custom scope | fee for creation + 15–30% share |
| Done-for-you fee | creator wants to own 100% | flat project fee, no share |
Define: what "net" means (after platform/payment fees, refunds, ad spend?), who owns the IP (usually creator owns, agency gets license/share), term (12–24 months incl. relaunches/evergreen), who pays tools and ads, reporting cadence, exit terms, approval rights, exclusivity.

## Proposal outline (`02-proposal.md`)
Their opportunity (audience data) → 3 product concepts (pick 1) → what we do / what you do → timeline → launch plan preview → revenue scenarios → terms → next step + signature.
Have a lawyer draft the actual agreement (see legal-and-compliance-basics).
