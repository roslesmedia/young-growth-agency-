# Creator → Product → Launch pipeline

The agency's job: partner with a creator, **create the product itself (content + design)**, and **help the creator launch it on their socials to maximize sales**. Every design decision derives from the creator — never from a house style.

## Operating rules
1. **Creator-first design.** Before any visual work, run `creator-brand-kit-extraction`. Templates in `ebook-pdf-production`, `social-carousel-design`, `product-mockups` are rendering scaffolds only — replace every color/font/layout choice with the creator's tokens and aesthetic.
2. **Audience language over our language.** Headlines, chapter titles and hooks reuse phrases mined from comments/DMs.
3. **Validate before building** (waitlist, poll, pre-sale) — never spend 40 hours on an unvalidated idea.
4. **Fast first version.** Ship a tight v1 (e.g. 30–60 page guide or 5-module mini course) at launch; expand in v2 using buyer feedback.
5. **Outcome-named products.** "The 30-Day Glow-Up Meal Plan" beats "Nutrition Guide".
6. **Track everything** from day 1 (UTMs per platform/post, discount codes per channel).
7. **Facts and claims:** verify statistics with sources; no income/health guarantees (see legal-and-compliance-basics).

## Kickoff questions for a new creator (ask the user)
Handle(s) + platforms • niche • follower counts & avg views • what they already sell • audience's top 3 questions • revenue split agreed? • launch window • creator's time availability (filming, lives) • brand assets they have (logo, fonts, photos).
