# Lead magnets & waitlists

## Good lead magnets (fast to consume, solve one slice of the paid product's problem)
Checklist • cheat sheet • 1-page plan • swipe file/scripts • mini template • 3–5 day email/DM challenge • quiz ("What's your <type>?") with segmented results • 1 free chapter • resource/tool list • short training video.
Rule: the freebie creates a quick win **and** reveals the need for the paid product ("now that you know what to eat, the 30-day plan tells you exactly when and how much").

## Waitlist page
Headline (product outcome) • 3 bullets of what's coming • launch date • founder perk (best price/bonus/early access only for the waitlist) • email (+ optional IG handle) form • creator photo • privacy note. Mobile-first, one field if possible. Thank-you page: "Reply/DM me your #1 struggle" + share prompt.

## Promotion
Comment-keyword reels (instagram-dm-automation-funnels), link sticker in stories daily, bio link, pinned post, collabs. Track signups per source.

## Pre-sale option
Sell founder spots at a discount with a delivery date to validate demand and fund production; communicate progress during creation (great content).

## Design
Lead magnets and waitlist pages are designed for the specific creator and product (brand kit) — they're the audience's first taste of product quality, so polish matters.
