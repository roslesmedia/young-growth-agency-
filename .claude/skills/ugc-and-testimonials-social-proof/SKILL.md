# Social proof & UGC

## Get proof before launch
- **Beta group**: give the product free to 10–30 engaged followers 1–2 weeks before launch in exchange for honest feedback + testimonial permission.
- **Creator's own results** and past client/follower wins (with permission).
- **Waitlist replies** ("I need this so bad") — excitement proof (only with permission, blur names if needed).

## Testimonial request script (DM/email)
"Hey <name>! So happy the guide helped 🙏 Would you be open to sharing a quick sentence or 15-sec video about: 1) where you were before, 2) what changed, 3) who you'd recommend it to? I may feature it on my page — I can blur your name if you prefer."
Prompting before/after/recommendation produces usable, specific testimonials.

## During launch (real-time proof loop)
Reshare buyer DMs and story tags (with permission), purchase-count milestones (true numbers only), first wins, "unboxing" screen recordings by buyers, live Q&A clips. Ask buyers in the delivery email to tag the creator.

## Placement
Sales page near every CTA and in pricing • story highlights "REVIEWS" • ads (UGC-style performs best) • carousels (BAB with testimonial) • emails (proof email) • checkout page.

## Ethics & compliance
Never fabricate or edit testimonials to change meaning; keep permission records; disclose if a reviewer got the product free or is affiliated; results claims must be typical or labeled ("results vary"); follow FTC Endorsement Guides / local equivalents.
