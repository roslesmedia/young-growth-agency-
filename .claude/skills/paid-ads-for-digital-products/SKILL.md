# Paid ads for creator digital products

## When ads make sense
- Amplify a launch: retarget the creator's warm audience (engagers, video viewers, page visitors, email list) — cheapest, highest ROAS.
- Scale a proven evergreen offer (sales page converting ≥ 2–3% from organic traffic).
- Don't run cold ads to an unproven offer.

## Formats
- **Meta partnership ads** (formerly branded content/whitelisting): run ads from the creator's handle using their organic posts — social proof + creator trust.
- **TikTok Spark Ads**: boost the creator's organic TikToks with an authorization code.
- Best creatives: the creator's top-performing organic reels/carousels, UGC testimonials, "what's inside" screen recordings, green-screen sales page walkthroughs.

## Structure (launch)
1. Warm retargeting campaign: audiences = IG/FB engagers 30–90d, video viewers 50%+, website visitors, email list (customer list). Exclude purchasers.
2. Waitlist/lead campaign during warm-up (optional).
3. Cold lookalike/broad interest test with 3–5 best creatives once pixel has purchase data.

## Tracking
Meta Pixel + Conversions API (via platform integration), TikTok Pixel, purchase events with value, UTMs. Check events fire in test mode before spending.

## Rules of thumb
- Know break-even CPA = price + bump/upsell average − fees (account for revenue share).
- Kill a creative after spend ≈ 1–2× target CPA with no purchase; scale winners 20–30% per day or duplicate.
- Refresh creatives when frequency climbs and CTR drops (creative fatigue).
- Daily launch budget scaled to warm audience size; most launch sales still come from organic + email — ads amplify.

## Compliance
Respect platform ad policies (no misleading income/health claims, before/after restrictions in some categories), mark paid partnerships.
