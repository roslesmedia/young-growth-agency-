# Creator discovery & fit scorecard

## Where to find creators
- Instagram/TikTok search + hashtags in niches where people buy info products: fitness, nutrition, personal finance, career, productivity, fashion/styling, beauty, photography/editing, design, cooking, parenting, language learning, business/marketing, mindset, travel planning, pets.
- "Comment KEYWORD" posts (they already think in funnels), creators asked repeatedly "how do you…?" in comments, YouTube creators with tutorial content, Substack/newsletters, LinkedIn educators.
- Sweet spot: **10k–300k followers** with high engagement — big enough to sell, small enough to want an agency partner.

## Data to collect (last 12–20 posts)
Followers, avg views (reels/TikToks), avg likes, comments, saves/shares if visible, posting frequency, % educational vs lifestyle content, existing link-in-bio offers, email list (ask), audience geo/age (ask for insights screenshots: top countries, age, gender).

## Key metrics
- **Engagement rate** = (likes + comments) / followers. IG: >3% good, >6% great (lower for 500k+). TikTok: use views/followers and comments/views.
- **Comment quality:** % of comments that are questions/requests ("where can I get this", "can you make a guide") vs emojis. Read 50+ comments.
- **Saves & shares** (if creator shares insights) → signals reference value = buyers of guides/templates.
- **View consistency:** median views vs follower count (median, not the viral outlier).
- **Trust signals:** face on camera, personal story, responds to comments, audience calls them by name.
- **Buyer intent:** audience has money + problem they'd pay to solve (e.g. "how do I land a job" > "funny dog clips").

## Red flags
Sudden follower spikes, comments from bots/engagement pods, giveaways-driven following, purely entertainment niche, already partnered with a competing product agency, recent controversies, misleading claims history, audience mostly very young (<18) for paid products.

## Scorecard (100 points)
| Criterion | Weight | 1 (poor) → 5 (excellent) guide |
|---|---|---|
| Audience–problem fit (painful, monetizable problem) | 20 | entertainment-only → urgent problem people pay for |
| Engagement quality (questions/requests in comments) | 15 | emojis → "please make a guide" |
| Trust & authority (face, story, results, credentials) | 15 | faceless reposts → known expert w/ proof |
| Reach consistency (median views ÷ followers) | 10 | <5% → >30% |
| Content–product bridge (teaches already) | 10 | none → tutorials every week |
| Audience purchasing power / geo | 10 | low → US/UK/CA/AU/EU, 25–45 |
| Existing monetization gap (nothing to sell or weak offer) | 10 | strong product exists → asks with nothing to buy |
| Collaboration factor (responsive, available to film/go live) | 10 | unavailable → excited, committed |
Score = Σ(rating/5 × weight). **≥75 go**, 60–74 go with conditions, <60 pass.

## Output template (`01-fit-scorecard.md`)
```
# <Creator> — Fit Scorecard  |  Score: 82/100 → GO
Platforms & stats: ...

```
Use web research for public stats; ask the user for anything behind logins. Be explicit about which numbers are estimates.
