# Legal & compliance basics (not legal advice)

Always recommend a qualified lawyer/accountant for contracts and tax. Use this list to flag issues.

## Partnership agreement should cover
Parties & scope (who creates what) • revenue split & definition of net • payment schedule & reporting • IP ownership (creator vs agency) and license to use materials • term, termination & what happens to the product after • approvals • confidentiality • non-disparagement • use of creator name/likeness • who pays tools/ads • refunds & chargebacks handling • exclusivity (if any) • dispute resolution.

## Content & IP
Original writing (no copying competitors); licensed images/fonts/music (stock licenses for commercial digital products; Google Fonts OFL is fine); no trademarked names in product titles; quotes attributed; templates sold with a clear license (personal use).

## Claims
- No guaranteed income, weight-loss, health or financial outcomes. Use "results vary", typical-results context.
- Health/fitness/nutrition: general education disclaimer, "consult a professional"; don't diagnose/treat.
- Finance: "not financial advice" disclaimer; no promised returns.
- Testimonials must be real, unedited in meaning, with disclosure of incentives.

## Disclosures
Creator posts promoting a product they co-own with the agency, affiliate links and paid partnerships must be disclosed clearly (#ad / "paid partnership" label / "I earn a commission") per FTC Endorsement Guides (US), ASA/CAP (UK) and local rules.

## Store & email
Refund policy and terms of sale visible • privacy policy (what data is collected, email, pixels) • cookie consent where required (EU/UK) • email: opt-in consent, unsubscribe link, sender physical address (CAN-SPAM), GDPR lawful basis for EU subscribers • age: avoid marketing paid products to minors.

## Tax
Digital products may require sales tax/VAT collection depending on buyer location — merchant-of-record platforms (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip for EU VAT, etc.) handle much of this; otherwise consult an accountant. Report revenue shares properly (invoices between agency and creator).

## Platform rules
Instagram/TikTok/Meta messaging and advertising policies; payment processor prohibited-content lists.
