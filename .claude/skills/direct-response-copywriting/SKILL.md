# Direct-response copywriting

## Frameworks
- **PAS** — Problem → Agitate → Solution. (Captions, ads, email openers.)
- **AIDA** — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action. (Sales pages, launch emails.)
- **BAB** — Before → After → Bridge (the product). (Testimonial posts, carousels.)
- **4Ps** — Promise → Picture → Proof → Push. (Long sales pages.)
- **Star-Story-Solution** — the creator (star) → their struggle story → the method/product.
- **FAB bullets** — Feature → Advantage → Benefit: "40 high-protein recipes (feature) under 15 minutes (advantage) so you hit your goals without meal-prepping all Sunday (benefit)".

## Bullet styles ("fascinations")
"The 2-minute fix for <problem> (page 14)" • "Why <common belief> is backwards — and what to do instead" • "Exactly what to say when <situation>" • "The <tool> I use every day (and the free alternative)".

## Objection handling map
| Objection | Answer with |
|---|---|
| No time | quick wins, time-per-day, short lessons |
| Too expensive | cost comparison, value stack, payment plan, guarantee |
| Won't work for me | segment-specific testimonials, "for beginners" proof, who it's for |
| Can find it free | curation, the creator's specific method, templates, time saved |
| Not now | deadline bonus, cost of waiting |

## CTA rules
One primary CTA per piece; outcome-oriented button copy; repeat CTA on long pages every 2–3 sections; P.S. in emails.

## Editing checklist
Remove filler & AI clichés • every sentence earns its place • "you" > "I/we" ratio • specific numbers • reading level ~grade 6–8 • creator voice pass • claims compliant and true.
