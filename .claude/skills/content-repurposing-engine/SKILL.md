# Content repurposing engine

## The pyramid
1 pillar asset (a product chapter, a 20–60 min live/podcast/YouTube video) →
- 5–10 short clips (Reels/TikTok/Shorts): best moments, each with a new hook and captions
- 3–5 carousels: frameworks, lists, myths from the pillar
- 3–5 text posts (LinkedIn / Threads / X): opinions, stories, lessons
- 1–2 emails: lesson + soft CTA
- 5–10 quote graphics / story frames
- Pinterest pins (for evergreen niches: recipes, fitness, planners, home, fashion)
- 1 blog/SEO article (optional)

## Workflow
1. Transcribe pillar (YouTube captions/Whisper) → mark timestamps of strong moments (story, contrarian take, step list, emotional peak).
2. Extract: hooks, frameworks, quotes, stats, stories, Q&As.
3. Rewrite natively per platform (don't copy-paste; hooks differ: TikTok casual, LinkedIn professional, Pinterest keyword-rich).
4. Design in the creator's style (social-carousel-design) and edit clips (captions, jump cuts, B-roll).
5. Schedule across 2–4 weeks; every piece ends with a product or lead-magnet CTA where natural.

## Product → content
Each product chapter yields: 1 tutorial reel (teaser of the method), 1 carousel (the framework without full depth), 1 myth post, 1 email. Give away the "what" and "why"; the product sells the "how", templates and the complete system.
