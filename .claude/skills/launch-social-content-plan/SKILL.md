# Launch & social content plan

## Content pillars (for the product topic)
1. **Teach** (value, how-tos, frameworks) → builds authority & saves
2. **Prove** (results, testimonials, case studies, before/after)
3. **Connect** (creator story, behind the scenes, opinions)
4. **Sell** (product reveals, what's inside, offers, FAQs)
Ratio: warm-up ~80/20 value:sell → open cart ~50/50 → after launch back to ~80/20 with evergreen CTAs.

## Angle bank (rotate during launch; each becomes reels, carousels, stories, emails)
Problem agitation • myth busting • the creator's origin story • "what I'd do if I started today" • tutorial excerpt from the product • a page/lesson preview • results/testimonials • who it's NOT for • price justification ("costs less than…") • objection answers • behind the scenes of making it • "day in the life using the product" • FAQs • urgency/countdown • buyer UGC reshares.

## Frequency during launch (typical creator)
Reels/TikToks: 1–2/day • carousels: 3–5/week • stories: 6–15 frames/day • lives: 1–3 per launch • emails per launch-email-sequences. Cross-post short-form to TikTok/Shorts/Facebook Reels with native captions.

## Reach multipliers
Collab posts (IG Collab feature) with complementary creators, guest lives, affiliates, trial reels (IG trial feature to test hooks with non-followers), pinned launch post on profile, highlight "GUIDE" with product info, bio link updated with UTM.
