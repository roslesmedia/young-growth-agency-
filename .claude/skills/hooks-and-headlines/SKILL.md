# Hooks & headlines

A hook earns the next 3 seconds; everything else is wasted without it. Hooks must be **specific**, **relevant to the audience's pain/desire**, and **paid off**.

## Formula bank
**Outcome**: "How I <result> in <timeframe> without <objection>" • "The <number>-minute <routine> that <result>" • "Do this before <common event>"
**Mistake/negative**: "Stop <common action> if you want <result>" • "<Number> mistakes keeping you from <goal>" • "You're <doing X> wrong"
**Curiosity**: "Nobody talks about this <niche> trick" • "I tried <thing> for 30 days — here's what happened" • "This is why your <thing> isn't working"
**Identity/call-out**: "If you're a <specific person> who <struggle>, watch this" • "POV: you finally <desired state>"
**Contrarian**: "<Popular advice> is ruining your <result>" • "Unpopular opinion: <stance>"
**Proof**: "How my follower went from <A> to <B>" • "What <$/result> actually looks like"
**List/save**: "Save this for your next <situation>" • "<Number> <tools/templates> I'd pay for (but are free)"
**Story**: "Two years ago I <low point>…" • "The day I almost quit <thing>"
**Question**: "Why does nobody tell you <truth>?"
**Product titles**: "The <Outcome> <Format>", "<Timeframe> to <Result>", "The <Named Method>"
**Sales headlines**: "<Dream outcome> — even if <biggest objection>" • "The exact <system> I used to <result>, now in a <format>"

## Rules
- Use the audience's exact words (language bank from creator-audience-research).
- Pair spoken hook + on-screen text + strong first visual (motion, face, result).
- Numbers and specifics beat vague claims; avoid clickbait you can't pay off.
- No unverifiable income or body-transformation guarantees.

## Testing
Write 5–10 hooks per idea, pick the top 3, test via IG trial reels / different posting times / carousel slide-1 variants; track 3-second hold rate and shares. Reuse winning hook structures with new topics.
