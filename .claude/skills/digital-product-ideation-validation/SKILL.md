# Product ideation & validation

## Format menu (match format to problem)
| Format | Best for | Price band (USD, typical) | Build time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mini guide / cheat sheet | one quick win | 7–19 | 1–3 days |
| Ebook / playbook (30–80 pp) | full method | 19–49 | 1–2 weeks |
| Workbook / planner / tracker (PDF/GoodNotes) | habits, planning, journaling | 15–39 | 1 week |
| Templates (Notion, Canva, Sheets, CapCut, Lightroom presets) | "do it for me" shortcuts | 19–79 | 3–10 days |
| Recipe/meal/workout plan | health/fitness | 19–59 | 1–2 weeks |
| Mini course (3–7 short videos + PDF) | skills needing demonstration | 49–199 | 2–3 weeks |
| Signature course | transformation | 199–997 | 4–8 weeks |
| Challenge (5–30 days, email/community) | momentum + upsell | 0–49 | 1 week |
| Bundle | max AOV | 2–3× single | reuse assets |

## Idea generation
From `03-audience-research.md`: for each top pain write "**[Outcome] in [timeframe] without [biggest objection]**". Generate 10–15 ideas across formats.

## Scoring (1–5 each, weight)
Demand evidence (×3) • creator authority on it (×2) • audience can afford it (×2) • speed to build (×1) • visual/"screenshot-able" for marketing (×1) • upsell/ladder potential (×1). Pick top 3 → present to creator.

## Validation (run 1–2 before building)
1. **Poll + question stickers**: "Would you want a <product> that <outcome>?" Aim ≥ 60–70% yes and many replies.
2. **Comment-keyword test**: post a reel about the problem with "Comment GUIDE for early access" → count comments (ManyChat collects emails). 100+ on a mid-size account = strong.
3. **Waitlist page** with early-bird discount; target ≥ 5–10% of reached viewers or a few hundred signups.
4. **Pre-sale** (best signal): sell at founder price with delivery date; 20–50 sales = build it. Refund if it doesn't ship.
5. **Mock cover test**: carousel with 3 cover/title options → "Which would you buy? 1, 2 or 3".

## Output (`06-product-ideas.md`)
Table of ideas + scores, top 3 one-pagers (name, promise, format, contents sketch, price, why now), chosen idea + validation results.
