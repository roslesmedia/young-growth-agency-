# Offer design & pricing

## Offer formula (Hormozi value equation, adapted)
Value ↑ with **dream outcome** and **perceived likelihood** (proof, creator's results); value ↓ with **time to result** and **effort**. So: promise a specific result, show proof, add quick wins, add templates/done-for-you elements.

## Naming
`The <Outcome> <Format>` / `<Timeframe> <Result> <Format>` / branded method (`The Soft Strength Method`). Test 3 names with the audience.

## Value stack
Core product + 2–4 bonuses that remove objections:
- "No time" → 5-minute quick-start / cheat sheet.
- "Don't know where to start" → checklist, 30-day plan.
- "Need it done for me" → templates, scripts, swipe files.
- "Need accountability" → challenge, tracker, private Q&A live.
Give each a sensible standalone value; total stack value 5–10× price (honest values only).

## Pricing
- Anchor: show the full stack value, then price. Use charm prices ($27, $37, $47, $97) for impulse products.
- Tiers (good/better/best): Essentials $27 • Complete (core + bonuses) $47 ← most pick • VIP (+ live call/feedback/community) $147. Middle tier is the target; VIP raises perceived value.
- Founder/early-bird price for waitlist (24–72h), then full price. Close cart or raise price at a real deadline.
- Order bump at checkout (30–50% of core price, complementary: e.g. "Canva templates pack +$17"); typical take rate 20–40%.
- One-click upsell after purchase (e.g. mini course $97), downsell if declined.
- Bundle after launch for higher AOV.
- Payment plans for $197+.

## Guarantee
30-day refund or "do the work" guarantee — reduces risk, rarely abused for low-ticket products. Make sure the platform/policy supports it.

## Scarcity & urgency (must be real)
Launch price ends at deadline; bonuses for first 100 buyers; cart close; live Q&A only for launch buyers. Never fake timers that reset.

## Output (`07-offer.md`)
Name, one-line promise, who it's for/not for, contents, bonuses (with values), tiers + prices, bump, upsell, guarantee, deadline mechanics, FAQ objections.
